The 1975

The 1975 are an English pop rock band formed in Wilmslow, Cheshire in 2002. The four schoolmates first came together performing covers of punk songs at local teen events before writing their own material, drawing from influences spanning synth-pop, indie rock, new wave, and electronic music. They won Best British Group at the Brit Awards in both 2017 and 2019, and have scored an unbroken streak of No. 1 albums on the UK Albums Chart. They're currently working on their sixth studio album.
