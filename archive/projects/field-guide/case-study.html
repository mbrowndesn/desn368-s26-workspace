A reflection on the design and development process behind Base Camp — a responsive field guide built for DESN368.

Design Decisions

What design choices did you make and why?

In this website design I really wanted to make the color palette and image aesthetics match the vibe of the outdoors and camping. I wanted the design choices to feel like they belonged out in the wilderness. The simple warm tones add to the feeling of the calming peaceful outdoors. For typography, Sigmar One has that bold, brush-stroke feel that anchors the BASE CAMP logo, while Patrick Hand's handwritten quality keeps everything else friendly and approachable.

Technical Challenges

What was difficult to code? How did you solve it?

The biggest challenge was getting the mountain silhouette to sit exactly on the green/orange seam, with its base resting on top of the orange section. It always seemed to sink to far down or float too far up, but through multiple attempts and help I was able to fix it. The other tricky moment was figuring out why a new section I added merged into a previous one. It turned out I had a missing closing div tag.

Responsive Strategy

How did you adapt your design for mobile?

I built the desktop layout first, then added in the mobile layout by using media queries for smaller screens. On mobile the fixed nav menu hides behind a pop out hamburger menu.

Learning Moments

What did you learn about Flexbox or CSS positioning?

I learned that position: fixed pins something to the screen no matter how you scroll, but it lifts the element out of the normal page flow, which is why I had to add margin-left: 13.5rem to push everything else over to clear it. For Flexbox, I learned that flex: 1 makes an item grow to fill the leftover space, which I used to make the article text take up the room next to a fixed-width photo.

Future Improvements

What would you add or change with more time?

For future improvements, I would love to add in scrolling animations and more distinct sections for better navigation and readability. I would also love to experiment with more bold design choices including backgrounds, icons, more fonts, etc.
