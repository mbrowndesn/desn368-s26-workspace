This is my coder manifesto

According to Claude: A coder manifesto is a short, bold declaration of the values and principles a programmer or community pledges to uphold in their work.

1) Build cool stuff

I will explore ideas even when I don't think they will work and go after fun ideas.

2) Let perfectionism go

I am learning and growing and things wont be perfect right away and I will learn to recieve feedback on unfinished projects.

3) My peers and teachers are a tool to be used

The people around me can help and grow me the most and I will ask for help and help others.

4) Stay curious

There is always a cooler way to do something and I want to find it.

5) Be okay with feeling stupid

I will not know everything right away and will feel like I'm stupid and that just means I'm learning and growing.
